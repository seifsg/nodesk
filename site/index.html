Break reminder for Windows

Breaks that follow your work, not the clock.

NoDesk lives in your tray and times micro, eye, rest and water breaks to your real keyboard and mouse activity. Step away and the timers pause. Stay away long enough and it counts as a break.

Download for Windows View source

Windows 10 and 11, x64 · free and open source

Small app, whole job

Counts real work

Once a second NoDesk reads your system idle time. Timers only move while you type or use the mouse.

Natural breaks count

Away from the desk as long as a break lasts? That break is done. No pointless reminder when you come back.

Four kinds of break

Micro-breaks to stretch, eye breaks for the 20-20-20 rule, rest breaks to walk, and water breaks.

One calm screen

Breaks due together share one full-screen countdown on every monitor, with a short tip for each.

Skip or postpone

Esc skips, P postpones 5 minutes. Keys in the first moment are ignored, so typing never dismisses a break.

Stays out of the way

Waits while a fullscreen app or presentation runs. Pause it from the tray whenever you need to.

Daily stats

Active time plus breaks taken, skipped and postponed, right in the tray menu. Kept on your PC.

Tiny and private

About 270 KB, under 2 MB of private memory at idle. No network, no camera, no account.

Questions

Windows warns me about the download

The exe is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway, or build it from source.

How do I start it with Windows?

Right-click the tray icon and tick Start with Windows. Untick it to remove it again.

Where are my stats stored?

In %APPDATA%\NoDesk\stats.tsv, one row per day. It opens in any spreadsheet and never leaves your PC.

Can I change the timings?

Not from the app yet. The defaults follow the 20-20-20 rule and common desk-work advice.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now.
